The lights in your home should do more than illuminate a dark room at night. They should inspire you, evolve with you, help you stay focused when it's needed, and help you relax after a long day's work. Ketra lighting design can help you achieve just that, completely changing the indoor living experience of your home. Here's how these tunable lighting fixtures can transform your daily lifestyle.

## The Power of Vibrancy

Vibrancy, a feature available only on Ketra products, is the unique ability to tune white light to reflect off objects the way you want, changing the way you perceive your home, your environment, and the valuable elements in it, such as artwork. Thanks to Ketra's patented LED optics system, it's possible to incorporate high-tech lighting solutions with a full spectrum of color that make colors pop while bringing out details that might otherwise go unnoticed.

This makes Vibrancy especially useful in rooms built around a focal point — a piece of art, a textured accent wall, a collection of furniture with rich material finishes. Instead of lighting a room in a single flat wash of white, Ketra fixtures can be tuned so the light itself does some of the design work, drawing attention to the details a homeowner actually wants seen.

## Warm Dimming for Every Hour of the Day

Do you ever find yourself trying to get work done under dim lights that just make you sleepy? Or have you tried to relax under bright, white lights that keep you wide awake when you're tired? Ketra's tunable full-spectrum light lets you enjoy a virtually infinite range of color and warmth to match the moment.

Typically, LED lighting provides only a single color temperature. Ketra's dynamic spectrum, however, allows for a wide range of customized colors, so you can set each space to any mood you like:

- Relax with a good book under a warm, candlelight-style glow.
- Stay alert and focused with a brighter, cooler tone during work hours.
- Experiment with a cool, crisp atmosphere when the moment calls for it.

With Ketra, the sky is the limit on how your lighting can support your day, rather than working against it.

## Getting Started

Because Vibrancy and warm dimming both depend on how fixtures are placed and tuned room by room, Ketra systems are best planned alongside the rest of your lighting design rather than bolted on afterward. A short consultation with an integrator can help identify which rooms will benefit most — living spaces and art-focused rooms tend to be natural starting points — before any fixtures are installed.
