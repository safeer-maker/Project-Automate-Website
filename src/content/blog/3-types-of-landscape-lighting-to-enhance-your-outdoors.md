Elevate the elegance of your design and extend your living space with landscape lighting. Technological advances offer real benefits for how homeowners use their property after dark — well-placed, well-controlled outdoor lighting can transform a yard into a genuine extension of your home, both for entertaining and everyday living. Here are three types of landscape lighting worth considering for your outdoor space.

## Functionality and Modernity: Match Lights

Match lights are one of the best ways to combine cutting-edge technology with a modern, minimalistic design. This watertight lighting solution is sealed to withstand inclement weather, letting you elevate your outdoor aesthetics with a clean, contemporary look rather than bulky fixtures that compete with your landscaping.

## Lighting That Decorates and Keeps You Safe: Step Lights

Step lights are a type of accent lighting that provides low-level illumination on steps, walls, and other vertical surfaces. Thanks to a rugged, patented cabling system, they're straightforward to install and offer a long lifespan — making them a great option for highlighting the luxury of your outdoor features while keeping you and your guests safe as they move around your property at night.

## Practical and Elegant: Bullet Lights

Bullet lights are small, elegant fixtures that blend into your landscape while delivering powerful illumination capable of showcasing your backyard's best features, from architectural details to specimen trees. These fixtures also offer a patented built-in adjustment, giving you full control over exactly where the light is directed.

## Bringing It All Together

Used together, match lights, step lights, and bullet lights let you layer illumination the way you'd layer lighting indoors — general, accent, and safety lighting each doing their own job. Pairing these fixtures with a smart home control system also means you can schedule your landscape lighting to turn on automatically as the sun goes down, so your property always looks its best without anyone having to flip a switch.
