Bring the fun outside with a Séura outdoor TV installation. There's nothing like watching an exciting sports event with friends, a film with your spouse, or a game night with the kids — and there's no reason that experience has to stay indoors. A proper outdoor TV lets you extend those moments to the patio, the pool deck, or wherever your outdoor living space happens to be.

## Why an Indoor TV Won't Cut It Outside

It might be tempting to simply mount a standard indoor TV outside, but indoor televisions aren't built for the rigors of an outdoor environment — sun exposure, temperature swings, and moisture will shorten their lifespan fast. High-end outdoor-rated displays, like those from Séura and Samsung's Terrace line, are purpose-built for life outdoors.

## Brightness That Competes with Daylight

Brightness is one of the most important factors when choosing an outdoor TV, since your screen has to compete with natural sunlight. Samsung's Terrace, for example, offers a high-definition 4K picture with brightness levels up to 2000 nits, letting you enjoy a genuinely sharp picture whether your setup is in partial shade or full sun.

## Consistent Picture Quality, Whatever the Conditions

A good outdoor TV should look just as good at high noon as it does after sunset. Adaptive picture systems automatically adjust brightness levels throughout the day to deliver a consistently strong image, no matter the time of day or the content you're watching — so you're not stuck fiddling with settings every time the light changes.

## Anti-Glare Technology for a Better Image

If you've ever avoided watching content outdoors on a sunny day because of screen glare, an outdoor TV solves that problem directly. Anti-reflective technology greatly reduces glare and enhances contrast, so you can enjoy the sunshine and your favorite content at the same time.

## Part of a Bigger Outdoor Entertainment Picture

A high-end outdoor TV is often just one piece of a well-designed outdoor entertainment space — pairing it with outdoor-rated audio and a shaded, comfortable seating area rounds out the experience.
