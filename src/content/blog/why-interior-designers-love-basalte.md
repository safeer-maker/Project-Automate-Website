Basalte is one of the most respected names in luxury home automation, and for good reason. These advanced systems integrate seamlessly with a wide range of smart home technologies and devices, while featuring beautifully designed interfaces that make it easy to control every device in a home from a single, centralized platform. Here's why interior designers keep coming back to Basalte for their projects.

## Elegant, Intuitive Control

Basalte offers several elegant ways for clients to control their smart home system. They can use beautifully designed wall panels, mobile apps, touchscreens, voice commands, and other intuitive control options to manage technology throughout the home. With Basalte's integrated control system, users can easily manage more than just entertainment — lighting, climate, motorized shades, security, and more can all be adjusted through a single, seamless interface.

## Designed to Disappear

While many homeowners focus primarily on functionality, interior designers understand that technology must also complement the aesthetic of a space. Bulky or unattractive equipment can disrupt the overall design of a room. Basalte technology is known for its sleek, minimalist design and premium materials, and its stylish wall panels and interfaces integrate effortlessly with modern interiors, allowing technology to blend naturally with the surrounding décor.

## Lighting That Sets the Scene

Lighting plays a crucial role in how a space feels, and interior designers know how important it is to incorporate lighting into their designs thoughtfully. Basalte makes it easy to manage lighting in a way that highlights architectural features, enhances décor, and creates the right atmosphere for any moment.

With Basalte, lighting can be tuned to the precise intensity and color needed for any occasion, and designers and homeowners can create preset scenes for different parts of the day:

- A "Cooking" scene might activate bright, functional lighting in the kitchen so a client can work comfortably.
- A "Dinner" scene could dim the lights and highlight the dining table with a warm glow to create the perfect ambiance for entertaining guests.

## Media at Your Fingertips

A Basalte system also lets clients control all of their favorite audio and video components from one centralized platform. Using a preferred smart device, homeowners can easily access streaming services, personal media collections, and multiple AV zones throughout the home with just a tap — opening an app, choosing a playlist, and instantly seeing what's playing in each room, then adjusting volume or moving music from one room to another through the same intuitive interface.

Favorite apps and media controls can also appear on Basalte touchscreen wall panels for quick access. Basalte systems can connect to in-wall and in-ceiling speakers, as well as displays that hide behind panels, rise from motorized lifts, or transform into artwork when not in use — allowing homeowners to enjoy a premium entertainment experience without disrupting the beauty of the space.
