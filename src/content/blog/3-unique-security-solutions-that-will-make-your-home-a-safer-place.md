Technological advances offer real benefits for everyday life, and smart home security is a great example. These systems help you effortlessly transform your home into a space of peace, safety, and comfort — and the best solutions go way beyond surveillance cameras. Here are three security solutions worth considering for your home.

## Alarm Systems

To make your home a truly safe place, you need a comprehensive system that protects you from both intruders and environmental risks. Integrating a state-of-the-art alarm system lets you monitor your home for temperature fluctuations, smoke and fire, hazardous gas leaks, and water leaks. The system notifies you the moment there's a problem, sending a text alert or push notification straight to your phone or another mobile device.

## Security Landscape Lighting

Landscape lighting is one of the best ways to maintain the safety of your outdoor space while boosting your home's curb appeal. Linking your landscape lighting to a smart home control system lets you schedule the outdoor lights to turn on automatically as the sun goes down. This helps dissuade potential intruders from targeting your home and helps prevent tripping accidents when you or your guests arrive home late at night.

## Smart Lock Systems

Never struggle to find the right key to lock or unlock your doors again. With a smart lock system, you can control your door locks from your smart device from anywhere in the world. Want an even simpler experience? Set up your system to automatically unlock when it detects your smart device nearby — a genuine step up in everyday convenience.

## A Layered Approach to Peace of Mind

None of these solutions have to work in isolation. An alarm system that shares a platform with your lighting and locks means one arm-away command can light your pathways, lock every door, and put your monitoring on alert — all at once, instead of juggling separate apps for each.
