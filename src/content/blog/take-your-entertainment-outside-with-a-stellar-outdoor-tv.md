Enjoying media content and entertainment outdoors is one of the greatest pleasures of warm weather. Whether you're planning a movie night, a cookout while watching the game, or even catching the news with a cup of coffee on a sunny morning, you need the right outdoor TV installation to have the best experience.

While it may be tempting to simply mount an indoor TV outside, we strongly advise against this — indoor TVs aren't equipped or designed for the rigors of an outdoor environment. Here's what actually makes a stellar outdoor TV experience.

## Enjoy Ultra-Bright Quality Picture

Brightness is one of the most important factors to consider when choosing an outdoor TV, since your screen's picture has to compete with natural sunlight. The Samsung Terrace is one of the best options on the market for an outdoor TV, offering a high-definition 4K picture with up to 2,000 nits of brightness, so you get a strong-quality picture whether you're set up in partial or full sun.

## The Environment Changes, But Your Quality Picture Remains

Have you ever been at a gathering so fun that you lost track of time? What starts as an afternoon cookout can easily turn into an evening cocktail hour. A properly installed outdoor TV keeps up with that shift — the Samsung Terrace's adaptive picture system automatically adjusts brightness levels to deliver a strong-quality image no matter the time of day or the content you're watching, so the experience stays consistent from your first guest to your last.

## Anti-Glare Technology for Better Image Contrast

If you've ever avoided watching content outdoors on a sunny day because of glare and poor image quality, an outdoor TV solves that problem. The Terrace is built with anti-reflective technology that greatly reduces glare on the screen and enhances contrast, so you can enjoy the sunny day and get some fresh air while still enjoying your home entertainment.

## Built to Handle the Elements

Beyond picture quality, a true outdoor TV is engineered to withstand the rigors of an outdoor environment — heat, humidity, dust, and temperature swings that would damage an indoor set over time. That durability is what allows the display to stay mounted year-round rather than being brought inside after every use, so it's ready whenever a movie night or game day comes together.
