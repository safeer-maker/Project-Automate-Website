What does it take to turn an ordinary outdoor space into an oasis of alfresco fun and relaxation? An outdoor entertainment system, of course. Whether you want to sit back and listen to relaxing music while stargazing on a clear night, host a brunch for friends on a sunny morning, or move your home office outside to breathe some fresh air while you work, the right technology makes it all possible.

## Enjoy Your Home Entertainment with an Outdoor TV

To enjoy outstanding outdoor entertainment, you need a high-quality outdoor TV. It may be tempting to simply take an indoor TV and mount it on a wall outside, but that will only cause trouble in the long run — indoor TVs aren't equipped or designed for the rigors of an outdoor environment.

Outdoor TVs are specially built to withstand harsh weather conditions, so whether there's a heatwave or a storm rolling through, you can be confident your TV will be safe and ready whenever you want to enjoy some outdoor entertainment. Outdoor TVs are also designed to offer a much brighter picture than indoor models, which helps reduce glare and lets you enjoy your content in partial or full sun.

## Turn Up the Music With High-Definition Outdoor Speakers

A high-performing outdoor audio system is a great way to enhance the fun and luxury of any outdoor activity. Whether you're hosting a party for friends, a cookout for family, or simply relaxing on your own after a long day, the right playlist in the background always sets the mood and creates a better experience.

Outdoor speakers are engineered to handle exposure to sun, moisture, and temperature swings without sacrificing sound quality, so they can be installed discreetly around a patio, pool, or garden and still deliver clear, even audio across the whole space — not just in a single spot near the speaker.

## Support It All with a Strong Networking System

A good entertainment system should always be supported by a robust network that lets you enjoy your content without interruptions or connectivity issues.
