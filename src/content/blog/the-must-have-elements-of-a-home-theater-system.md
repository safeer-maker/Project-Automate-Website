There's nothing like watching an exciting sports event with friends, a romantic film with your spouse, or playing video games with your kids. In all of these activities, there are two common denominators: you're spending time with the people you love, and you're enjoying great entertainment. Read on to discover three must-have elements for the best possible content-watching experience at home.

## Surround Sound System

A world-class sound system is essential for a truly immersive experience. Whether you're watching an NFL game or an Oscar-winning film, the right audio installation pulls you right into the world on your screen instead of leaving you watching it from a distance.

## High-Definition Projector

A crisp picture, lifelike colors, and perfect definition are just a few of the things you need to enjoy your content the way it deserves to be seen. At PROJECT: automate, we partner with Barco Projectors to provide powerful installations that make you feel like you're at the movie theater — without leaving home.

## Home Automation System

Home entertainment isn't just about what's on your screen — it's a whole experience. With a smart home automation system, you can quickly customize your surroundings to make that experience more comfortable. Close your blackout shades, dim your lights, arm your security system, and set the perfect temperature, all with the press of a button or a simple voice command.

## Designing the Room Around the Experience

The best home theaters treat these elements as one integrated system rather than separate purchases. A projector and sound system that are tuned to the room, and lighting and shades that respond automatically when the movie starts, add up to something that feels far more polished than any single component on its own.
