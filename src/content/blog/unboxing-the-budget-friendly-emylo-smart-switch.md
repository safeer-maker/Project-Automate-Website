The eMylo smart switch is a compact WiFi relay module that lets you control lights and appliances remotely using a smartphone app, voice commands, or an RF remote. It's one of the most popular entry points into budget smart home automation — over three million people worldwide use eMylo products, with the brand shipping more than seven million units across 137 countries.

## How It Works

At its core, the eMylo switch is an in-line smart relay wired directly into the electrical line feeding a light fixture or appliance. When you send a command from your phone or voice assistant, the relay opens or closes the circuit. It runs on the Tuya IoT ecosystem and integrates natively with the Smart Life app, letting you set schedules, build scenes, and connect it into broader smart home routines.

Some models add a 433MHz RF receiver alongside WiFi. That gives you a physical remote that works completely independently of your home network — useful if your internet or WiFi goes down, since the RF signal talks directly to the relay.

## What It Takes to Install

Most eMylo relays use spring-loaded lever terminal blocks rather than screw terminals, so wiring takes about five seconds per connection instead of requiring a screwdriver. A standard 1-channel install looks like this:

- Turn off power at the breaker and verify with a voltage tester
- Identify the live and neutral wires from your power supply, and the corresponding wires to the fixture
- Connect the incoming live and neutral wires to the "Input" terminals
- Connect the outgoing wires to the fixture to the "Output" terminals
- Tuck the module into the junction box or fixture canopy

Setup runs through the Smart Life or Tuya Smart app: connect your phone to your home's 2.4GHz network (5GHz isn't supported), put the switch into pairing mode by holding its button until the LED blinks quickly, then add the device and enter your WiFi credentials.

## Specs and Real-World Ratings

Standard eMylo relays are rated for 10A / 2200W at 220V — about 1100W on a 110V system — enough for LED lighting, lamps, and small appliances, but not for high-draw equipment like space heaters or pumps. The eMylo Smart WiFi Light Switch 2-Pack holds a 4.2-star rating from 141 Amazon reviews, roughly in line with the SONOFF Basic R2 (4.2 stars, 364 ratings) and a step behind higher-end budget competitors like the SONOFF 3-Pack (4.4 stars) and Shelly 1 Mini Gen3 (4.6 stars).

## Where It Fits, and Where It Doesn't

The appeal is real: eMylo relays are inexpensive, genuinely easy to wire, and stable thanks to the mature Tuya/Smart Life ecosystem. For a single lamp, a holiday light display, or a first step into home automation, they do the job well.

The tradeoffs show up at scale. The plastic housing feels a step below premium hardware, the 2.4GHz-only WiFi can cause pairing headaches on modern mesh routers that auto-steer devices to 5GHz, and control runs through Tuya's cloud rather than a local API — which can add latency and raises privacy questions for anyone who wants local-only control. Fitting the module into a shallow or crowded junction box, especially in an older home, can also be tighter than expected.

For a room or two of simple lamps, a device like this is a reasonable way to try automation. For a home with complex lighting zones, integrated AV, and premium finishes, scattering dozens of individual WiFi relays throughout the walls tends to create more clutter and network congestion than it solves — which is where a centralized, professionally engineered lighting system starts to make more sense. If you're weighing DIY relays against a whole-home approach, it's worth a conversation before you commit to either path.
