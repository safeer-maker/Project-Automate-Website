The consumer demand for home technology and automated integration is advancing at an unprecedented rate. Today, a home on the market with a fully-integrated automated system gets noticed quickly and sells fast, and these systems are increasingly factored into the appraisal for home sales.

As a result, more design-build firms are recognizing the value of including smart home automation. Some incorporate different automated packages directly into their contracts, giving them a competitive edge in a rapidly growing sector. These design-build professionals now include smart home integrators on their team, in much the same way plumbers and electricians play a part in the overall plan. At PROJECT: automate, we're a home automation company committed to delivering best-in-class service, working with leading industry brands to provide a remarkable, luxurious ease of living.

## Peace of Mind

Working with certified professionals who have 20+ years of experience brings a peace of mind that doesn't come with providers selling smart home kits or DIY installs — approaches that take more time, lead to more headaches, and can result in disparate systems that require multiple remotes to control.

Working with an experienced, professional integrator ensures all automated systems operate from one easy-to-use interface. Lighting, shades, security, climate, door locks, gate entry systems, outdoor entertainment, and audio-video are all controlled from a single, user-friendly platform. As certified home automation integrators, we take the time to speak with your clients directly. Learning about their lifestyle and daily patterns helps us create solutions that genuinely make their lives easier and their homes simple to manage, and lets us customize systems around how they actually live.
