If your smart home feels more like a collection of disconnected gadgets than a seamless experience, you're not alone. Most homeowners end up juggling multiple apps, a voice assistant on the counter, and switches that only do one thing. Brilliant smart switches were designed to solve exactly that — in-wall touchscreen panels that replace standard light switches and act as a central hub for lighting, music, cameras, locks, and climate, all from one device.

## How the System Works

At its core, the Brilliant system combines two types of hardware: a primary Control Panel and companion Smart Dimmer Switches. The main panel features a 5-inch touchscreen and connects to your home's 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network to talk to cloud-based smart home systems, streaming cameras, and thermostats. To extend control to other rooms without installing an expensive touchscreen everywhere, the Smart Dimmer Switches communicate with the main panel over a Bluetooth Low Energy mesh network, with each dimmer acting as a repeater. As long as dimmers stay within roughly 50 feet of each other or the panel, they hold a reliable local connection independent of Wi-Fi.

A simple swipe on the panel adjusts the lights, but because it's networked, that same gesture can dim Philips Hue bulbs, adjust Sonos volume, or trigger a "Goodnight" scene that locks the front door and arms the security system.

## What's Built Into the Panel

- **Built-in Amazon Alexa** — an integrated microphone and speaker mean voice commands without another speaker cluttering the counter.
- **Video intercom and camera** — multiple panels can be used for room-to-room video calls, with a physical privacy shutter to cover the lens when it's not needed.
- **Motion detection** — the screen wakes automatically when you walk in, and can dim to act as a digital photo frame when the room is empty.

The companion Smart Dimmer Switch supports up to 500W of incandescent or halogen lighting, or up to 200W of LED/CFL, with capacitive touch sliders for dimming, a double-tap gesture to trigger scenes, and its own motion sensor. It also plays well with smart bulbs like Philips Hue or LIFX, maintaining constant power to the bulb while sending wireless dimming commands — which avoids the common problem of a smart bulb going unresponsive after someone flips the physical switch.

## What to Check Before You Buy

Wired Control Panels and Smart Dimmer Switches require a standard 120V AC electrical system with both a neutral wire and a ground wire present in the box. Most homes built from the mid-1980s onward have neutral wires throughout; older, unrenovated homes may need an electrician to run them before installation. For homes or apartments where that isn't possible, a Plug-In Control Panel mounts flush to any wall and draws power from a standard outlet, requiring no electrical work at all — a useful option for renters or historic properties.

## Where Brilliant Fits

Brilliant occupies a middle ground between basic DIY smart switches and professional, luxury lighting control systems. It consolidates far more into one interface than a standard smart switch, with a touchscreen and dimmers replacing one to four separate switches and native integrations across Sonos, Ring, Philips Hue, Ecobee, and smart locks. Luxury architectural systems go further still, hiding the actual control hardware in a closet or equipment rack and using minimalist keypads on the wall instead of a screen — a different aesthetic tradeoff worth weighing depending on the space.

## Is It Right for Your Home?

For homeowners who want a clean, unified way for family and guests to control the house without needing a smartphone app, Brilliant is a genuinely strong option — and for builders, it's an easy way to add a premium, tech-forward feature during construction. Whether a modular system like Brilliant or a fully custom architectural lighting system is the better fit depends on your home, your wiring, and how you want the walls to look. If you're not sure which direction makes sense, it's worth talking it through with an integrator before you buy anything.
