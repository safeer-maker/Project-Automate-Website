## Experience the World's Most Powerful Residential Projector

Barco projectors are some of the most powerful projectors in the world. Used in both residential and commercial applications, they change the way you experience movies.

The hand-built Thor+, for example, uses an integrated 6P RGB laser light source that offers 4K 2D and 3D projection on white and silver screens with minimized laser speckle. Thor+ also features Barco's analog color convergence mechanism, which achieves precise convergence through manually adjustable green and red DMD chips that render images sharper. The projector provides 30,000 hours of life with at least 80% of its initial brightness — built to last.

## Enjoy the Unmatched Benefits of CinemaScope

When you watch a movie in a commercial theater, the image is most often shown in an ultrawide 2.39:1 format, also known as CinemaScope. Barco projectors are among the few that bring this format into residential theaters. CinemaScope technology uses automatic aspect ratio detection to take the viewing experience further — the projector analyzes incoming sources, detects the native resolution, and automatically scales it to fit. You can easily switch between formats and set a preferred aspect ratio, even when the source material has a menu outside the frame.
