A customized alarm system installation can help you create a secure environment for your family and enjoy your home to the fullest, offering greater safety for the people and property that matter most.

Having someone disrupt the peace of your home is one of the greatest concerns for homeowners. That's where an integrated smart security system comes in. ## Let Your Smart Home Be Part of the Magic

The rest of your smart home can help keep you safe too. With an Alarm.com smart home system, you can:

- Control and program your lighting to make it look like you're home.
- Set motorized shades to close automatically when the sun goes down.
- Turn on landscape lighting in the evening to discourage potential intruders.
- Control the locks on your doors remotely.

Manage all of it through the Alarm.com app and effortlessly keep your home safe.

## Beware of Environmental Hazards

Keeping your home and family safe is about more than external risks. Fire, carbon monoxide, and water leaks are also common threats that can seriously endanger the people you care about. A smart security system lets you monitor these hazards through a user-friendly app on your smartphone — through your alarm system, it's possible to shut off your water supply or pinpoint the source of danger in case of a fire, with the system able to automatically alert emergency services and get professional help on the way quickly.

## One System, Full Coverage

The real value of a top-level alarm system is that it doesn't treat intrusion, fire, and water damage as separate problems requiring separate equipment. Cameras, door and window sensors, environmental monitors, and the rest of your smart home all report back to the same Alarm.com platform, which means one app, one set of notifications, and one place to check when you want to know that everything at home is exactly as it should be.
