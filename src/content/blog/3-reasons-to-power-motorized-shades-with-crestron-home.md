One of the most convenient and stylish ways you can implement smart technology into your home is by upgrading to motorized shades that open and close on preset schedules or with just a button tap. Motorized shades add everyday convenience to your routines and value to your Los Angeles property.

Even better, Crestron motorized shades paired with Crestron Home automation present you with an array of benefits, from personalized scenes to custom fabrics and styles. Here are three reasons to consider Crestron for motorized shades and smart control.

## Battery-Powered Options

Motorized shades come in wired and wireless models, and there are benefits to both. While wired shades do not require replacing or recharging batteries, they are difficult to install in an already-built home since they require opening walls for long wire runs.

Alternatively, battery-powered shades can easily be added to a home without tearing open the walls to run wiring. Crestron recently added battery-powered shades to their portfolio, and they're powered by durable D-Cell batteries that last three years on average.

Battery-powered shades by Crestron are smart, beautiful, and help meet the needs of all homeowners. Plus, when paired with a Crestron Home system, Crestron will keep track of usage to alert you when it's time for a battery replacement.

## Customization

Your motorized shades should enhance your interior design, not detract from it. When you choose Crestron shades, you can select from over 400 fabrics, including 240 designer styles, making it easy to find a customized look that complements your décor.

Crestron Color Match takes this a step further by allowing you to choose colors and fabrics that exactly complement your space. Depending on the use of each room, you can install shades that are blackout, translucent, or transparent to manage the amount of light that comes into the room.

## Automated Control

Crestron brings so much more than motorized shades to your home. When you pair your shades with Crestron Home automation, you can integrate all your home technology into one platform and automate your shades, lights, climate, and more to adjust on their own based on your routines.

Coordinate your lights and shades to keep your home cool on hot days and create smart home scenes for different activities and weather conditions. Easily control your shades and more through your smartphone, tablet, Horizon keypad, or a voice control system.

If you're considering motorized shades for your home, it's worth talking through Crestron's options with an experienced integrator before choosing fabrics or hardware — a short consultation can help you get the fit, function, and automation right the first time.
