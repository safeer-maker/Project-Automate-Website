Why the Brilliant Smart Switch is a Bright Idea for Your Home

The Brilliant Smart Switch: A Smarter Way to Control Your Home

Brilliant smart switches are in-wall touchscreen control panels that replace standard light switches and act as a central hub for your entire smart home — controlling lighting, music, cameras, locks, and climate from one device.

Here is a quick overview of what makes them stand out:

Feature Detail
Control panel types 1-switch to 4-switch wired panels, plug-in panel
Smart Dimmer Switch price $69.99 single, as low as $59.99 in bundles
Control panel price range $299 to $749 depending on model
Voice assistants supported Amazon Alexa (built-in), Google Assistant, Apple HomeKit
Key integrations Sonos, Ring, Philips Hue, Ecobee, August Lock, SmartThings
Wiring required Neutral wire and ground wire (120V AC, US only)
Connectivity Wi-Fi + Bluetooth Low Energy mesh network
Works without Wi-Fi? Yes, for local lighting control

If your smart home feels more like a collection of disconnected gadgets than a seamless experience, you are not alone. Most homeowners end up juggling multiple apps, voice assistants on countertops, and switches that only do one thing. The Brilliant system was designed to solve exactly that — putting everything behind a single, intuitive touchscreen mounted right where you already reach for the light switch.

That said, Brilliant is not the only option. Depending on your home and your expectations, it may be a perfect fit — or just a starting point. This guide breaks down everything you need to know.

I’m Joshua Trevithick, Founder and CEO of PROJECT: automate, and with over two decades designing integrated smart homes across Southern California, I’ve evaluated the Brilliant smart switch alongside every major platform on the market. I’ll give you an honest, experience-based look at where Brilliant excels and where a more advanced system might serve you better.

What is the Brilliant Smart Switch and How Does It Work?

At its core, the brilliant smart switch is an in-wall touchscreen control panel designed to replace a standard light switch. Instead of just turning a single light bulb on and off, it serves as a physical, visual bridge for your entire house. Think of it as a localized smart home tablet that lives permanently in your wall, wired directly into your home’s electrical grid.

The system works by combining two distinct types of hardware: the primary Control Panels and the companion Smart Dimmer Switches.

The primary control unit, known as the Brilliant smart home control, features a vibrant 5-inch diagonal LCD touchscreen with a 720×1280 resolution. It connects directly to your home’s 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network to communicate with cloud-based smart home systems, streaming cameras, and smart thermostats.

To expand this control to other rooms without putting an expensive touchscreen in every single wall, Brilliant uses companion Smart Dimmer Switches. These dimmers communicate with the main control panel using an automatic hopping Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Mesh network. This means each dimmer switch acts as a repeater, passing signals along to the next switch. As long as your dimmers are placed within approximately 50 feet (roughly two rooms) of each other or the main panel, they maintain a rock-solid local connection, completely independent of your Wi-Fi signal.

When you walk up to a Brilliant panel, a simple swipe of your finger up or down on the screen adjusts the lights. But because the panel is connected to your local network, that same swipe can be customized to dim your Philips Hue bulbs, adjust your Sonos volume, or trigger a custom “Goodnight” scene that locks your front door and arms your security system.

Key Features and Ecosystem Integrations of Brilliant

The true power of the brilliant smart switch lies in its ability to consolidate multiple smart home systems into one physical interface. If you live in Malibu, Beverly Hills, or Manhattan Beach, you likely have a mix of high-end appliances, security systems, and entertainment setups. Keeping them all organized can be a full-time job.

With a centralized interface, you can manage these disparate technologies from a single wall point. For a broader look at how centralized hardware ties your home together, you can explore our guide on Control Systems.

Let’s explore how the specific components of the Brilliant ecosystem work together.

Centralized Control with the Brilliant Smart Switch Panel

The main Control Panel is the brain of the operation. Available in configurations that fit into 1-gang, 2-gang, 3-gang, or 4-gang electrical boxes, it directly replaces your existing light switches.

Key features built into every main panel include:

  • Built-in Amazon Alexa: The panel features an integrated microphone and speaker, allowing you to use voice commands without cluttering your kitchen countertops with smart speakers.
  • Video Intercom and Camera: A built-in camera allows for room-to-room video intercom calls between multiple Brilliant panels. If you want to call the kids down for dinner in your Hollywood Hills home, you can do it right from the kitchen wall.
  • Physical Privacy Shutter: For peace of mind, a physical slide switch allows you to completely cover the camera lens and disable the microphone.
  • Motion Detection: The panel uses built-in motion sensors to automatically turn the screen on when you walk into a room, and turn it off (or dim it to act as a digital photo frame) when the room is empty.

Expanding Your Network with the Brilliant Smart Switch Dimmer

The Brilliant Smart Dimmer Switch is the workhorse of the system. Priced at $69.99 (and as low as $59.99 in multi-packs), it allows you to bring smart lighting control to every room in your house without buying a touchscreen for every wall.

These dimmers support up to 500W of incandescent/halogen lighting or up to 200W of LED/CFL lighting. They feature capacitive touch sliders, allowing you to dim the lights by sliding your finger up or down the physical switch.

Additionally, the dimmer includes:

  • Double-Tap Scene Activation: You can program a double-tap on the physical switch to trigger custom scenes. For example, double-tapping the bedroom switch can turn off all house lights, set the thermostat, and lock the doors.
  • Built-in Motion Sensors: Like the main panel, each dimmer has a motion sensor that can be configured to turn lights on when you enter and off after a set period of inactivity.
  • Smart Bulb Compatibility: If you use smart bulbs like Philips Hue or LIFX, you can configure the Brilliant switch to maintain constant power to the bulb while sending wireless dimming commands. This prevents the common issue of smart bulbs becoming unresponsive when someone flips the physical wall switch.

To understand how advanced dimmer configurations can elevate your interior design and lighting layers, read our comprehensive overview of Light Control.

Installation Requirements and Setup Process

Installing a brilliant smart switch is designed to be a straightforward DIY project for those comfortable with basic electrical work, though professional installation is always recommended for multi-gang boxes or complex configurations.

Before you purchase, you must verify your home’s wiring. The wired Brilliant Control Panels and Smart Dimmer Switches require a standard 120V AC electrical system and must have both a neutral wire and a ground wire present in the electrical box.

Most homes built in the mid-1980s or later in areas like Venice Beach, Pacific Palisades, or Studio City will have neutral wires throughout. However, if you live in an older, historic home in Los Angeles, you may need to consult an electrician to pull neutral wires to your switch boxes.

The physical installation process generally follows these steps:

  1. Turn off the power: Always shut off the circuit breaker controlling the switch before starting.
  2. Remove the old switch: Unscrew the existing switch and take a picture of the wiring configuration for reference.
  3. Connect the wires: Wire the Brilliant baseplate using the provided wire connectors. The switch is intelligent enough to automatically identify line and load wires, but you must manually connect the neutral and ground wires.
  4. Mount the baseplate: Screw the base plate into the electrical box.
  5. Attach the faceplate: Snap the touchscreen faceplate onto the baseplate.
  6. Configure on-screen: Turn the power back on. The touchscreen will boot up and guide you through connecting to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, testing your lights for flicker, and linking your third-party smart home accounts.

For official, step-by-step documentation and videos on this process, you can refer to the Brilliant – Installation Smart Home Control page.

Who is Brilliant Best Suited For?

  • Tech-Savvy Homeowners: If you love smart home technology but want a clean, unified way for family members and guests to control the house without needing a smartphone app, Brilliant is an exceptional solution.
  • Modern Builders: For builders constructing new homes in Rancho Palos Verdes or Rolling Hills, installing Brilliant panels during construction adds a premium, high-tech feature that instantly appeals to modern buyers.
  • Renters (Plug-In Option): The Plug-In Control Panel mounts flush to any wall and plugs into a standard outlet via a 13-foot USB cord. It requires no electrical wiring or neutral wires, making it perfect for luxury apartments in Hermosa Beach or Redondo Beach where modifying the electrical system is not allowed.

Comparing Brilliant to Traditional and Luxury Smart Switches

While the brilliant smart switch is an impressive consumer-grade solution, it occupies a middle ground between basic DIY smart switches (like Lutron Caséta or standard Z-Wave switches) and professional, luxury lighting control systems like Lutron HomeWorks or Savant.

If you are designing a high-end estate in Bel Air or Beverly Hills, you may want to consider how Brilliant compares to a bespoke architectural solution. Luxury systems focus on “invisible intelligence”—hiding bulky equipment in remote closets and using elegant, minimalist keypads on the wall rather than glowing touchscreens in every room.

Frequently Asked Questions about Smart Lighting Control

Does the Brilliant Smart Dimmer Switch require a control panel?

Yes. The Brilliant Smart Dimmer Switch does not have its own Wi-Fi radio. Instead, it relies on a local Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Mesh network to communicate. To function as a smart device, at least one wired or plug-in Brilliant Control Panel must be installed in your home. The dimmer switches must be placed within approximately 50 feet (or two rooms) of the control panel or another dimmer node to maintain a reliable connection.

Will my Brilliant switches work if the Wi-Fi goes down?

Yes, but with limitations. Because the switches communicate with each other locally via a Bluetooth mesh network, your physical light switches, dimming functions, and motion sensors will continue to work perfectly even if your internet service provider goes down. However, any features that require an internet connection—such as viewing your Ring doorbell feed, adjusting your Nest thermostat, playing Sonos music, using Alexa voice control, or controlling your lights remotely via the mobile app—will be unavailable until your Wi-Fi is restored.

Can I install Brilliant switches in an older home without a neutral wire?

If you want to use the standard, in-wall wired Control Panels or Smart Dimmer Switches, neutral and ground wires are strictly required. If your home was built before the mid-1980s and has not been rewired, you likely do not have neutral wires behind your switches.

However, you still have options:

  1. The Plug-In Control Panel: You can purchase the Brilliant Plug-In Panel, which mounts to any wall without an electrical box and plugs into a standard outlet. This allows you to use the touchscreen interface to control wireless smart bulbs (like Philips Hue) and smart plugs throughout your home without needing neutral wires in your walls.
  2. Professional Rewiring: You can work with a professional team to run neutral wires to key switch locations in your home, allowing you to install the fully integrated wired system.

Conclusion

The brilliant smart switch is an exceptional, highly innovative solution for homeowners who want to clean up their walls, eliminate countertop clutter, and establish an intuitive, family-friendly control point for their smart devices. By replacing standard switches with elegant touchscreens, Brilliant makes daily interactions with your home feel seamless and modern.

However, for luxury estates in neighborhoods like Malibu, Beverly Hills, or Palos Verdes, a touchscreen on every wall isn’t always the right aesthetic choice. True luxury automation is about “invisible intelligence”—technology that works flawlessly in the background, leaving your home’s architecture and interior design to take center stage.
